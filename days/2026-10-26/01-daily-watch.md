## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. The rural-stress story moved from one lender's call to an industry theme. Shriram, Mahindra Finance, L&T Finance and HDB are all lending more cautiously in drought pockets, and two rating agencies have named microfinance as a pressure point.**
- Mint's lead this morning (26 Oct, 06:00 IST) puts the Q2 calls side by side. The monsoon ended at **87% of LPA, the weakest since 2015**. September CPI rose to **5.54%** (4.83% in August), with food inflation at **7.74%** (5.97%). ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/nbfcs-turn-cautious-on-rural-lending-as-weak-monsoon-threatens-repayments-11792921724354.html))
- **Shriram Finance:** Umesh Revankar named **Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana** as the worst-hit states where it has a sizable presence. GS2 is **7.04%** (6.96% in Q1) and credit cost is **2.03%** (1.68% a year ago).
- **Mahindra Finance:** Raul Rebello said **282 districts** have been classified as drought-prone, and the company has cut funding in some stressed markets. InCred says tractor disbursements fell **20% QoQ**. Its asset quality still improved, with GS3 at 3.4% (3.9%).
- **L&T Finance:** rural disbursements were **₹6,356 cr, −9% QoQ** (+1% YoY). The rural book is ₹33,465 cr (+22% YoY).
- The agencies disagree on timing:
  - **Jinay Gala (India Ratings):** "Microfinance, unsecured business loans and, to some extent, tractor financing could face pressure… from December onwards."
  - **Sanjay Agarwal (CareEdge):** it is more likely to show in **Q4 than Q3**, and "may not show up in microfinance just yet".
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):**
  - Two of Shriram's three worst-hit states, Karnataka and Telangana, are in MFL's southern footprint.
  - For the gold book, a weak harvest usually means *more* demand for gold loans, not fewer. That is my read, not a sourced forecast. The catch is that it is distress demand: renewals slow and the chance of an auction rises if gold keeps falling (see #2).
  - The exposure that is actually monsoon-sensitive sits in MFL's non-gold retail books and in the **Muthoot Microfin** JLG book on a consolidated basis (see the deep dive).
  - Before the Q2 pack closes, cut Stage 2 and collection efficiency by district for Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana, using Mahindra's drought-district list as the filter.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Monday: Microfinance & SHG lending

### The microfinance recovery is real, but it is still narrow, and the drought is the first test

**1. The portfolio has bottomed but isn't growing with new borrowers.** Infomerics' October 2026 outlook draws on CRIF High Mark. ([Infomerics](https://infomericstorage.blob.core.windows.net/uploads/mfi_report_oct26_bbfd906019.pdf))
- Industry GLP fell from **₹3.81 lakh cr** (Mar-25) to **₹3.21 lakh cr** (Dec-25), then rose to **₹3.33 lakh cr** (Jun-26), which is **−7.3% YoY** and +0.6% QoQ.
- Live borrowers fell from **8.0 cr to 6.6 cr** over the year, while the average ticket size of new loans rose to **₹62,000**.
- **57%** of originations went to repeat borrowers.
- In short, the recovery is coming from larger loans to repeat customers.

**2. Asset quality is clean, for now** (Infomerics/CRIF).
- **PAR 1–180** fell from **7.6%** (Mar-25) to **2.3%** (Jun-26), and PAR 91–180 to **0.8%**.
- Borrowers with **two or fewer lenders** are now **80.9%**, and those with five or more lenders **1.1%**.
- The guardrails are working. The open question is what happens to these ratios when farm incomes take an 87%-of-LPA hit.

**3. The listed MFIs are growing again and diversifying out of JLG.**
