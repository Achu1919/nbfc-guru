Source for everything below: **Reserve Bank of India (Non-Banking Financial Companies – Prudential Norms on Capital Adequacy) Directions, 2025**, `RBI/DOR/2025-26/345`, `DOR.CAP.REC.264/21-01-002/2025-26`, dated 28 Nov 2025, as updated to 1 Jul 2026. ([RBI MD](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12959))

### What is it?
A Middle Layer NBFC's Tier 1 isn't simply its net worth. RBI starts from **owned fund** (para 9) and then strips out three kinds of exposure that look like capital on the balance sheet but wouldn't absorb a loss in a stress:
1. **Money put into your own group** above a 10% allowance (para 10(i)).
2. **Profit booked upfront on co-lent loans** that hasn't been earned yet (para 15(2)).
3. **Default-loss guarantees you've given** (para 15(3)).

### The Technical Details

**A. Start: owned fund (para 9)**
- **Includes:** paid-up equity, CCPS, free reserves, share premium, and capital reserves from sale proceeds (not revaluation).
- **Quarterly profits** count only if limited-reviewed or audited, net of an average-dividend adjustment. Current-year losses come off in full.
- **Then deduct:** accumulated losses, the book value of intangibles, and deferred revenue expenditure. Right-of-use assets on tangible leases are **not** deducted.

**B. The group haircut (para 10(i))**
For a gold-loan NBFC or a Middle Layer NBFC, Tier 1 is owned fund **reduced by the amount by which the aggregate of the following exceeds 10% of owned fund**:
- (a) investments in **shares of other NBFCs**; and
- (b) investments in **shares, debentures, bonds, outstanding loans and advances (including hire purchase and lease finance), and deposits** with **subsidiaries and companies in the same group**.

Points that catch people out:
- **It's one combined bucket.** Equity in a subsidiary, an NCD you hold in it, a loan you've given it and margin money parked with a group company all add up together. The MD's notes say margin money with a subsidiary or group company counts as a deposit.
- **Only the excess above 10% is deducted**, not the whole exposure.
- **Ind AS (para 16(2)):** value these investments and advances at the **lower of cost or fair value**.
- The same 10% rule applies to Upper Layer CET1 (para 8(3)). For MFL, as Middle Layer, it bites at Tier 1.

**C. Co-lending's unrealised profit (para 15(2))**
- If a co-lending arrangement books **unrealised profit** upfront (as defined under the Transfer and Distribution of Credit Risk Directions, 2025), that amount is **deducted from CET1 / net owned fund until the underlying loans mature**.
- This matters because the P&L shows a gain today, while the capital ratio has to wait for the loans to run off.

**D. Default-loss guarantees (para 15(3))**
- An NBFC that **provides a DLG** deducts the **full outstanding DLG amount** from capital. It isn't risk-weighted; it comes straight off.
- (For completeness, para 15(1) covers subordinated AIF units, which are deducted from both Tier 1 and Tier 2.)

**E. Illustration (hypothetical numbers only; these are not MFL figures)**

| Step | ₹ cr |
|------|------|
| Owned fund (after intangibles, DRE) | 1,000 |
| Group bucket: equity in subsidiaries 70 + loans to group 40 + NCDs in group 10 + margin money with group 5 | 125 |
| 10% allowance | 100 |
| **Para 10(i) deduction (excess)** | **25** |
| Unrealised co-lending profit outstanding (para 15(2)) | 6 |
| DLG given and outstanding (para 15(3)) | 4 |
| **Tier 1** | **965** |

On risk-weighted assets of ₹6,000 cr, Tier 1 falls from **16.7%** (1,000/6,000) to **16.1%** (965/6,000). That's about 60 bp, from exposures that never show up as a "loss".

### Why It Matters
- The 12% Tier 1 / 15% CRAR floors in para 6(1) are tested **after** these deductions. A group that keeps funding its subsidiaries through the parent can lose capital headroom without any credit event.
- Rating agencies and NCD investors read the same reported CRAR. Its movement between quarters has to be explainable, and so does the deduction line.

### Key Numbers

| Item | Number |
|------|--------|
| Gold-loan NBFC floors (para 6(1)) | **Tier 1 12%, CRAR 15%**; Tier 2 ≤ 100% of Tier 1 |
| Middle Layer Tier 1 (para 6(2)) | **10%** (the gold-loan 12% applies to MFL) |
| Group/NBFC investment allowance (para 10(i)) | **10% of owned fund**, with the excess deducted |
| Valuation (para 16(2)) | **Lower of cost or fair value** |
| Unrealised co-lending profit (para 15(2)) | Deducted **until the loans mature** |
| DLG given (para 15(3)) | **100%** of the outstanding amount deducted |
| Base Layer leverage cap (para 17) | ≤ **7×** (not MFL; Middle Layer) |

### Quiz yourself
1. MFL has ₹300 cr of equity in a subsidiary and ₹150 cr of loans to it, against an owned fund of ₹4,000 cr. How much is deducted under para 10(i)? *(Bucket ₹450 cr − allowance ₹400 cr = **₹50 cr**.)*
2. A group company's shares are carried at a cost of ₹100 cr, with a fair value of ₹80 cr. Which value goes into the bucket under Ind AS? *(**₹80 cr**, the lower of cost or fair value, under para 16(2).)*
3. A co-lending deal books ₹5 cr of gain upfront on loans maturing in 12 months. When does that ₹5 cr count as capital? *(Only **as the loans mature**. Until then it's deducted under para 15(2).)*
4. Is a DLG given risk-weighted at 100% or deducted? *(**Deducted in full** under para 15(3).)*
5. Does margin money placed with a group company count toward the 10% bucket? *(**Yes.** The MD's notes treat it as a deposit with the group company.)*
