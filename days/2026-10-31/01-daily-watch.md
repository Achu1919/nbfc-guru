## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Gold had its worst day in months on Friday, and the gold-loan stocks went down with it.**
- Spot gold fell **$122.06 (−3.04%) to $3,899.33** on Friday, below $3,900 for the first time since mid-July. Silver fell 6% to an 11-month low. MCX gold was at **₹1,38,386/10g** by 23:42 IST. The triggers were hot US inflation, strong GDP, high oil and bets on a December Fed hike, and gold was heading for a monthly loss of more than 4%. ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/commodities/gold-silver-prices-crash-mcx-gold-plunges-to-rs-1-lakh-silver-falls-to-rs-1-lakh-per-kg-20023794.htm), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/30/gold-slips-as-higher-oil-us-inflation-data-bolster-fed-hike-bets.html))
- Spot gold was **$3,875.36 (−2.93%)** at 07:04 IST today. ([exa.ai XAUUSD](https://exa.ai/library/markets/commodity/gold?currency=USD))
- Listed gold financiers fell on the day: Manappuram **−4.34%**, Muthoot Finance (peer, **not** MFL) **−3.54%**, IIFL Finance **−6.15%**. No company-specific trigger was reported; the move lines up with the gold sell-off, but no source ties the two directly. ([exa.ai NSE closes](https://exa.ai/library/markets/stock/MANAPPURAM?exchange=XNSE&date=2026-10-30&view=daily), [Global Banking & Finance](https://www.globalbankingandfinance.com/manappuram-finance-manappuram-ns-falls-2026-10-30/))
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** a 3% overnight drop eats straight into LTV headroom on loans written near the peak. On Thursday MCX was ₹1,42,351/10g; it's now about 2.8% lower. Loans sanctioned close to the 75% cap in the last few weeks are the ones to run through the LTV-breach report on Monday, before Q2 numbers are frozen.

**2. MFL's Tranche I NCD reached 1.86× on Friday, with about ₹372 cr in against the ₹500 cr full size.**
- Subscription at 30 Oct, 17:00: **1.86×** overall; retail 1.60×, HNI 2.56×, non-institutional 1.29×, institutional 0.00×. It was 1.67× on Thursday. The issue is **₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe**, from a ₹4,000 cr shelf, allotted first-come first-served, and closes **Thu 5 Nov**. ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** the daily pace held at about 0.19× (≈₹38 cr) on Friday. At that rate the book gets to roughly ₹500 cr around the close, so the full green shoe is in reach but not banked. Retail is doing the heavy lifting; there's still nothing from institutions.

**3. Brent settled at a six-month high after a tanker was mined in the Strait of Hormuz.**
- The expiring December Brent contract settled at **$117.64 (+$2.76, +2.4%)** on 30 Oct; the January contract settled at $113.43. Brent was up 13.6% for October. ([Reuters via Euronext](https://live.euronext.com/en/financial-news/oil-jumps-2-settles-6-month-highs-fears-escalation-iran))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** oil is the main reason markets now price a December Fed hike, and the same oil shock feeds Indian inflation. ABCL said on Friday its incremental borrowing cost rose 11 bp in Q2 and will rise another 5–10 bp in Q3 ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/aditya-birla-capital-expects-510-bps-rise-in-q3-funding-cost-creates-overlay-for-macro-risks/135378014)). MFL's own bank-line and NCD costs will move the same way.

**4. Nomura flagged four headwinds for NBFCs and prefers urban lenders over rural ones.**
- The headwinds: weather stress on the rural economy (monsoon deficit of 13%, six states have declared drought), food inflation hitting urban customers, a longer rate-hike cycle, and rules on flexi loans and insurance. Its bear case assumes the risk-free rate rising to 7.25% from 6.25% and a 50% cut in insurance fee income from FY28. Top picks: Piramal Finance, Tata Capital, Aadhar Housing, Bajaj Finance. ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/stocks/nomura-flags-four-headwinds-for-nbfcs-prefers-piramal-finance-tata-capital-aadhar-housing-finance-and-bajaj-finance-14064543.html))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** the drought states Nomura names (Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka, Telangana and others) are in MFL's southern catchment. Gold loans usually do well when rural cash is short, since borrowers pledge jewellery instead of skipping payments. But a falling gold price plus rural stress is the mix that tests auction and collection numbers.

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Saturday: Infrastructure & project finance

**1. The state-owned infra lenders are growing, but profits are mixed.**
- **REC (26 Oct):** Q2 FY27 PAT **₹3,478 cr, down 21.2%**; NII down 9% to ₹5,478 cr. Its renewable book grew 27% to about ₹86,660 cr, and non-power infrastructure and logistics is now about ₹61,637 cr, more than 10% of loans. REC also ran India's first pilot tokenised corporate bond: ₹500 cr, bids of about ₹796 cr, under SEBI's sandbox. ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/rec-share-price-q2-results-net-profit-revenue-declares-interim-dividend-20019558.htm))
- **IREDA (19 Oct):** Q2 PAT ₹554 cr (from ₹549 cr); disbursements +31% to ₹10,579 cr; loan book ₹97,411 cr (+15%); net NPA 1.08% against 1.97%. ([ETEnergyworld/PTI](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/renewable/ireda-net-profit-grows-to-554-cr-in-september-quarter/135061370))

**2. PFC–REC merger is heading to Cabinet.** RBI gave its no-objection on 15 Oct, NSE and BSE issued observation letters on 19 Oct, and the government is weighing preference shares to keep its 51% stake. The target date is 1 April 2027. ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/rec-share-price-q2-results-net-profit-revenue-declares-interim-dividend-20019558.htm))

**3. IIFCL is getting ready for an IPO.** Its 28 Oct board approved restated financials for the IPO and noted the appointment of book-running lead managers. It also approved taking over illiquid and defaulted securities left in the old IIFCL Mutual Fund infrastructure debt fund, following a SEBI direction. ([NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/debt/WDM/IIFCL3_28102026183202_Outcome_of_Board_Meeting__3_.pdf))

**4. NaBFID is building a credit bureau for projects.** The Finance Ministry and RBI approved a National Infrastructure Data Platform, a single database of project data from concept to completion, drawing on NHAI, banks and Gati Shakti. RFPs for a consultant and a technology partner are out. ([ET, 21 Oct](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/infrastructure/nabfid-gets-finmin-rbi-nod-for-national-infrastructure-project-repository/articleshow/135096343.cms))

**5. Banks are lending hard into infrastructure.** RBI's September data show non-food bank credit up 19.1% year on year, industry up 19.5%, with infrastructure among the "buoyant" segments. ([RBI PR 2026-2027/1412](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63891))

**6. Read for Muthoot Fincorp:**
- **Competition for bond money:** AAA infra lenders, a merged PFC–REC and a listed IIFCL will be in the debt market all year. They set the floor that MFL's AA paper is priced against, so wider PSU spreads would push MFL's cost up too.
- **Bank appetite:** banks are lending to infra and NBFCs at the same time. For now that keeps MFL's bank lines open, but long-tenor infra loans use up the same bank balance-sheet room.
- **Second-order benefit:** infra build-out means construction jobs and contractor cash flow in MFL's semi-urban markets, which supports MSME and gold-loan demand.
