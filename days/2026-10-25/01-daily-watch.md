- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** MFL's equity isn't listed, so there's no share-price hit. But the market is clearly pricing collateral risk into gold lenders. MCX gold is now more than 20% below this year's ₹1.81 lakh peak (BusinessLine quoting Aditya Agarwala). That shrinks the LTV cushion on loans written near the top, and it raises auction odds on the oldest buckets. Run the LTV-migration table at ₹1.40 lakh and ₹1.35 lakh (my scenarios, not a forecast) before the Q2 board pack goes out. CARE notes that gold was about 67% of consolidated AUM at March 2026, all of it on one-year tenure. ([CARE](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202610121058_Muthoot_Fincorp_Limited.pdf)) ([BusinessLine live blog, 23 Oct](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-23rd-october-2026/article71611889.ece))

**2. Shriram Finance says it will keep pushing gold loans despite rising stress. It blames an EMI repayment format, and its book LTV is about 72–73%.**
- On the Q2 call, Executive Vice Chairman Umesh Revankar said the plan to double gold from about 2.5% to 5% of assets is unchanged. The gold book is about **₹9,000 cr**. Q2 gold disbursements were **₹5,443 cr** (₹5,153 cr in Q1). LTV including accrued interest is **about 72–73%**. Management puts the Stage 2/3 rise down to new **EMI and interest-servicing gold products**: borrowers are used to bullet repayment. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/why-shriram-finance-wants-to-double-down-on-gold-loans-despite-repayment-red-flags-12228710))
- Revankar also cut FY27 growth guidance to **15–17%** (from 18%), citing the West Asia war and a monsoon that ended at 87% of LPA. He said credit cost could run 30–40 bps above the 2% guidance for a quarter or two. Consolidated Q2 PAT was **₹3,684 cr (+59%)**. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/why-shriram-finance-wants-to-double-down-on-gold-loans-despite-repayment-red-flags-12228710), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/quarterly-results/shriram-finance-q2-profit-jumps-59-to-3-684-crore-on-auto-demand-growth-126102301480_1.html))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** "Customers aren't used to EMIs" is worth noting. If peers' Stage 2 is inflating because of product design rather than borrower distress, any MFL gold products with EMI or periodic interest servicing should have their Q2 Stage 2 split by **repayment type** (bullet vs EMI/interest-servicing) before analysts lump it into "gold stress". A diversified lender sitting at 72–73% LTV with a stated plan to double its gold share is also a pricing competitor in MFL's catchments.

**1. The gold trade turned this week.** Gold fell 3.6% (spot) and MCX December dropped from ₹1,47,300 to about ₹1.42 lakh. Gold-lender stocks fell up to 6% on Wednesday and again on Friday.
