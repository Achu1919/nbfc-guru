**B. The idea that links this week: when does a gold loan become "overdue"?**

Under IRAC an amount is overdue when it isn't paid by its due date, and a loan becomes an NPA when it stays overdue past 90 days. Apply that to two products:

- **Bullet gold loan (12 months):** the only due date is at maturity, so nothing can fall overdue for a year. A bad borrower stays standard until day 365 + 90 unless the LTV breach is enforced first.
- **EMI / monthly-interest gold loan:** every month is a due date, so a missed instalment starts the 90-day clock straight away.

Same customer, same gold, very different Stage 2/3 timing. That is the regulatory mechanics behind Shriram's line that customers "used to bullet repayment" haven't been servicing on time ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/why-shriram-finance-wants-to-double-down-on-gold-loans-despite-repayment-red-flags-12228710)). It's also why comparing gold GS3 across lenders without the product mix is close to meaningless.

### Why It Matters
A quiet regulatory week is when product design and existing rules do the damage. Peers' gold stress this week came from how the IRAC clock meets repayment design, not from a new circular. The RBI Bulletin's NBFC review (a staff study, logged 24 Oct) said NBFCs lost share even as profitability and asset quality improved ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/nbfcs-lose-ground-despite-stronger-profitability-better-asset-quality-rbi-study/article71617268.ece)). ### Quiz yourself
1. Two gold borrowers both stop paying in May. One has a 12-month bullet loan sanctioned in January, the other a 12-month monthly-interest loan sanctioned in January. Which one can be an NPA in Q2 FY27 (Jul–Sep), and why?
2. Gold falls another 5% and a branch wants to run its first auction online at an 85% reserve "because the market is falling". What two things are wrong?
3. Tranche I closes on 5 Nov with ₹175 cr of valid bids. Valid or failed, and what does FP&A plug into the November ALM?

*(Answers: (1) The monthly-interest loan. Its May instalment falls overdue, so it crosses 90 days in August and becomes an NPA in Q2, with the borrower's other facilities dragged along. The bullet loan has no due date until January next year, so it stays standard unless the LTV breach is enforced. (2) The first auction must be physical in the same district, and the reserve must be ≥90% on the first attempt; 85% is allowed only after two failed auctions. (3) Valid, because it's above the ₹150 cr minimum (75% of the ₹200 cr base). Plug in ₹175 cr (subject to final allotment), not ₹200 cr or ₹500 cr.)*
