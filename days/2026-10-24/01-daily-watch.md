## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Shriram Finance: profit +59%, but gold-loan Stage 3 jumped and the stock fell. Gold-loan stress now has a fourth large NBFC behind it.**
- Q2 FY27 standalone PAT **₹3,677 cr (+59.4% YoY)**, ahead of the LSEG consensus of ₹3,546 cr. AUM **₹3.29 lakh cr (+17%)**, NIM **9.52%** (8.19% a year ago), disbursements **₹54,079 cr (+25.7%)**. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/shriram-finance-q2-profit-jumps-59-yoy-to-rs-3677-crore-on-strong-interest-income-lower-funding-costs/articleshow/135150195.cms), [VCCircle/Reuters](https://www.vccircle.com/shriramfinance-joins-peers-in-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans))
- Gold loans of **₹8,974 cr (+68.2%)** were the fastest-growing segment. Gold-loan **gross Stage 3 rose to 3.12%**, from 2.57% in Q1 and 2.28% a year ago. Company GS3 4.65% / NS3 2.31%, PCR 51.48%, credit cost 2.03% (1.68% a year ago). ([VCCircle/Reuters](https://www.vccircle.com/shriramfinance-joins-peers-in-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/earnings/shriram-finance-q2-results-shares-as-gross-net-stage-3-assets-rise-dividend-declared-20018042.htm))
- The stock settled about **4% lower at ₹865** and was the top Nifty loser. Shriram follows Bajaj Finance (Stage 2+3 up ₹1,601 cr in Q2, of which ₹1,047 cr was gold) and Piramal ("15–23% of industry gold disbursements went to customers who had been 90+ DPD at least once"). ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/gold-loan-stress-nbfc-piramal-bajaj-finance-precious-metal-11792580129138.html))
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** Shriram is now a **₹9,000 cr gold lender that is still growing at about 68%**. That pressures pricing on MFL's own branch catchments. A rising gold Stage 3 at a diversified lender also makes analysts ask the same question about every gold book, and MFL's Q2 numbers will be read against it. Fitch's view (via Mint) is that LTV cushions limit credit cost. The real risk is **auction volume and provisioning timing**. MFL has to answer that with its own data, not a peer's.

**2. MFL (employer): Tranche I public NCD is open. Day 1 showed 0.55× subscribed.**
- Tranche I (prospectus dated 19 Oct) **opened 23 Oct and closes 5 Nov**. Size is **₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe = ₹500 cr** within the ₹4,000 cr shelf. Coupons run **8.56%–9.25%** (effective yield 8.89–9.25%) across 24/36/60/72 months. Rated **CRISIL AA/Stable and BWR AA+/Stable**; BSE listing. Proceeds go to onward lending and debt repayment. ([FintechBizNews](https://www.fintechbiznews.com/exchanges-ipos-nfos/muthoot-fincorp-rolls-out-ncd-tranche-i-aims-to-raise-rs5-bn), [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/), [Nuvama prospectus](https://www.nuvama.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Muthoot-FinCorp-Limited-Tranche-I-Prospectus-dated-October-19-2026.pdf))
- Chittorgarh's tracker showed **0.55× subscription at the end of day 1**. The issue was opened on a day when Nifty Financial Services fell 1% and the rupee hit 94.93. ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/))
- **Read:** this tranche closes **before** SEBI's Credit Risk-o-Meter goes live (21 Nov). The next MFL tranche after that date will carry a chartreuse "Very low credit risk" meter, driven by its lowest rating (AA). See the 7 AM report.

**3. RBI Governor: Upper Layer NBFCs must list, and "public funds" means public funds. He backed RBI's position on Tata Sons.**
- At the NDTV World Summit on 23 Oct, Governor Malhotra said that UL NBFCs and CICs with access to public funds have to be registered, and that UL entities have to list. That backs the classification of Tata Sons, whose surrender application RBI rejected last month. He also said RBI's inflation projection averages about 5.1% and that RBI targets no particular rupee level. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/company/corporate-trends/rbi-governor-backs-tata-sons-classification-says-large-nbfcs-must-follow-listing-norms/articleshow/135149254.cms), [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/rbi-listing-requirement-nbfcs-499056))
- **Speech, not a circular.** MFL is **Middle Layer** and is not on the 2026-27 UL list, so the listing mandate does not bite on MFL. Muthoot Finance (peer) **is** UL and is already listed.

**4. RBI Bulletin review of NBFCs: profits up, asset quality better, but share of financial-system assets down. Gold-jewellery loans up nearly 60%.**
- The October 2026 RBI Bulletin article "Performance of the NBFC Sector: Review for 2025" found that NBFCs' share of financial-sector assets slipped to **12.7% (Mar 2025)** from 13.9% (Mar 2020). UL+ML assets were **+19.4%** and loans **+24%** at Dec 2025. Gold-jewellery loans were up **nearly 60% YoY**. Borrowings rose **23.3%**, and over 77% of them came from banks and market instruments. GNPA was **2.7%**, NNPA **0.9%**, CRAR **25.2%**. The article flags bank interconnectedness. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/nbfcs-lose-ground-despite-stronger-profitability-better-asset-quality-rbi-study/article71617268.ece), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/industry/banking/nbfcs-reliance-on-bank-borrowings-rises-amid-strong-credit-growth-rbi-126102301611_1.html), [RBI PR](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63859))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** a Bulletin article is research, not regulation. But the "bank borrowings and interconnectedness" theme is exactly what supervisors cite before tightening bank-to-NBFC risk weights. MFL's liability mix (bank lines vs retail NCDs) is the sensitivity to keep updated.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Infrastructure & Project Finance *(Saturday focus)*

- **PFC–REC is the structural story.** RBI gave its NoC on 15 Oct. If shareholders and creditors approve, the merged entity would have a **loan book above ₹11 lakh cr**. The swap ratio is 88 PFC shares for every 100 REC shares. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/rbi-gives-noc-to-pfc-rec-merger/article71591605.ece))
- **PFC keeps incubating transmission SPVs.** It has incorporated Solapur SEZ and Karur REZ transmission SPVs, plus an SPV for a **₹4,378 cr Northern Region STATCOM** scheme. The SPVs are bid out to developers. ([Kalkine](https://www.kalkine.co.in/article/announcements/power-finance-corporation-nsepfc-why-did-it-incorporate-two-new-transmission-spvs), [PowerPeak Digest](https://powerpeakdigest.com/pfc-northern-region-grid-statcom-spv/))
- **Renewables credit is still growing fast.** IREDA's disbursements were +31% and its loan book +15%, with NNPA falling to 1.08%. ([Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/energy-infrastructure/power-and-renewable-energy/ireda-net-profit-rises-to-rs.-5.54-bn-in-q2-fy27/99244))
- **Read for MFL:** none of this competes with MFL's book. The indirect link is **bond-market supply**. PFC, REC and IREDA issue AAA paper in size. With repo at 5.50% and Brent at $114, heavy PSU-NBFC issuance keeps the AA spread that MFL pays on its retail NCDs from compressing.
