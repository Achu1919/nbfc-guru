# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Wednesday, October 28, 2026

*Wednesday, Oct 28, 2026 edition · News & Markets desk. Indian prices are Tuesday's (27 Oct) close. Gold spot and MCX gold are this morning's early-Asia prints, and Brent is Tuesday's settlement. Regulatory deep-dives live in NBFC Guru's 7 AM report.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Gold sits about 20% below its January record, and analysts have started putting peer LTVs side by side. The SGB redemption price RBI set for today says the same thing.**
- BusinessToday (27 Oct) says gold is roughly **20% below its record of ₹1,76,306/10g** set on 29 Jan, and it opened Tuesday at **₹1,40,664**. Equirus's Shreepal Doshi lists LTVs of **Muthoot Finance (peer, not MFL) 64%** (Q1), **Manappuram 65.6%**, **Fedbank 68%** (Q2), **IIFL 70%** (Q2) and **Capri Global 73.35%** (Q1), calling Capri's position "less comfortable". Gold-loan yields have slipped to **75–79 bps a month**, down from 100–105 bps. ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/gold-prices-correct-jewellers-may-gain-but-will-gold-loan-growth-and-lender-margins-take-a-hit-564084-2026-10-27))
- The RBI fixed the premature redemption price of **SGB 2020-21 Series I**, due today, at **₹14,159 per gram**. That is the simple average of IBJA 999 closing prices on 23, 26 and 27 Oct. ([RBI press release](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63872))
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** MFL isn't on the Equirus list, so analysts will ask for its number. Illustrative arithmetic: a loan written at 75% LTV at the January peak is now at about **94%** of current gold value (75 ÷ 0.80). Before the Q2 call, MFL should have three things ready: its portfolio LTV, the share of the book above 80% at today's price, and the vintage of those loans.

**2. NBFCs are pulling back from drought-hit rural districts. The south is the worst-hit region.**
- **Shriram, M&M Finance and Bajaj Finance** are curbing fresh loans in districts hit by the weak monsoon. The season ended **13% below normal**, the weakest since 2015. Rainfall was **−24% in the southern peninsula** and **−32% in Karnataka**. Shriram's Parag Sharma said it may revise LTV norms. M&M Finance is watching 3 states and 282 drought-prone districts. Bajaj is tracking 720 districts and has cut MFI originations in some of them. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/nbfcs-curb-fresh-loans-in-rural-markets-as-weak-monsoon-raises-repayment-risks/articleshow/135222291.cms))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** MFL is Kerala-based with a large southern network, and its subsidiary Muthoot Microfin also lends in the south. Map MFL's gold and non-gold disbursements against the IMD deficit districts, especially in Karnataka. If large lenders pull back there, MFL either picks up the business or picks up the risk, and that should be a deliberate choice.

**3. Tata Capital's PAT jumped 51%, and it is passing the repo hike through: floating rates go up 20–25 bps from 1 Nov.**
- Q2 FY27 PAT was **₹1,660 cr** (₹1,097 cr a year ago). AUM is **₹3.08 lakh cr (+26%)** and NII **₹3,696 cr (+23%)**. Asset quality: GS3 **1.9%**, NS3 0.8%, PCR 58.6%. Capital: CRAR 17.3%, Tier 1 14.7%. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/tata-capital-q2-net-profit-rises-51-on-higher-income/articleshow/135239343.cms), [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/tata-capitals-consolidated-q2-net-profit-jumps-51-to-1660-cr/article71632266.ece))
- Management said floating-rate loans will be repriced **20–25 bps higher from 1 Nov** after the RBI's rate hike. Revolving and flexi loans, the products the RBI's August draft would bar, make up **3–4% of AUM**. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/tata-capital-q2-net-profit-rises-51-on-higher-income/articleshow/135239343.cms))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** the large lenders are repricing their books upward. MFL's gold loans are largely short-tenor, so its main lever is pricing on new loans and renewals. Its Tranche I NCD coupons of 8.56–9.25% are a live read on the marginal cost that pricing has to cover.

**4. MFL's own Tranche I NCD has crossed its base: 1.25× at Tuesday's close.**
- Subscription was **1.25×** at 27 Oct, 17:00: retail 1.02×, HNI 1.74×, non-institutional 0.90×, institutional 0.00×. It was 0.99× on Monday. The issue is **₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe**, allotted first-come first-served, and closes **Thu 5 Nov**. ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** the base is done, with seven bidding days left. The issue now runs on the green shoe. HNI demand is leading, while retail just crossed 1×. Institutional demand is still nil, which is normal for this kind of issue.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Wednesday: Housing finance

### Affordable HFCs are still growing fast. The repo hike is the test.

**1. Home First is the first affordable HFC to report Q2, and its numbers held up** ([investor presentation](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/c80dce47-aa17-4ab5-bccf-0ef6fb28f70d.pdf), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/home-first-finance-q2-results-share-price-net-profit-rises-29-pc-nii-up-asset-quality-cfo-20020584.htm)):

| Metric | Q2 FY27 |
|---|---|
| AUM | ₹18,010 cr (+27.0% YoY) |
| Disbursements | ₹1,705 cr (a record quarter) |
| PAT | ₹170.2 cr (+29.1% YoY, +6.5% QoQ) |
| RoA / RoE | 4.2% / 14.9% |
| 1+ DPD / 30+ DPD | 4.7% / 3.2% |
| GNPA / NNPA / Stage 3 PCR | 1.74% / 1.34% / 23.6% |
| Credit cost | 40 bps |
| Cost of borrowing / marginal COB | 7.8% / 7.5% |
| Origination yield (ex co-lending) | 13.0% |

**2. Margins are where the pressure shows.** Spreads excluding co-lending narrowed **10 bps** in the quarter. Home First's last prime-lending-rate (PLR) move was a **10 bp cut in January 2026**, and there has been no hike since the RBI raised rates. Its marginal cost of borrowing (7.5%) is still below its book cost (7.8%), which gives it some room for now. Stage 3 PCR of 23.6% is thin and relies on property collateral. ([investor presentation](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/c80dce47-aa17-4ab5-bccf-0ef6fb28f70d.pdf))

**3. The sector backdrop:**
- The RBI's NBFC review in its October Bulletin puts housing credit growth at **12.1%** (Aug 2026). That compares with **22%** for retail overall and **69.1%** for gold-jewellery loans. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-shift-towards-secured-retail-credit-as-microfinance-loans-decline/135153797))
- The Nifty Housing Finance index rose **+0.63%** on a day when financials fell. ([NSE](https://archives.nseindia.com/content/indices/ind_close_all_27102026.csv))
- **PNB Housing** reports tonight and **LIC Housing** on 30 Oct. Watch both for repricing: whether they pass on the rate hike or absorb it.

**What it means for MFL:**
- **Cross-sell.** Housing isn't a core MFL product. Home First's numbers show that a granular, low-ticket housing book can grow 27% with a 40 bp credit cost. That is a useful benchmark if housing referrals or LAP come up in MFL's FY28 plan.
- **Pricing discipline.** Home First's spread narrowed by 10 bps without any rate shock to its own book. MFL's gold-loan yields are under similar pressure (the 75–79 bps a month cited in #1), so its renewal pricing needs to be reviewed this month, not after Q3.
- **Unverified item:** a bankcreds.com post claims Manappuram and Muthoot Finance "jump 8% on October 27". Exchange data shows **Manappuram −0.37%** and **Muthoot Finance +0.49%** on 27 Oct. It is **unverified and not used**.
