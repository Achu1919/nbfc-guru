# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Monday, October 26, 2026

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. The market punished NBFCs that guided to higher credit costs or thinner margins. Shriram fell to an 11-month low and Five-Star dropped 7.45%, even though both reported profit growth.**
- **Shriram Finance** cut its FY27 AUM growth guidance to **15–17% from 18%**, citing the weak monsoon and geopolitical uncertainty, and flagged stress among borrowers in drought-hit regions. The stock fell as much as **6.13% to ₹810.20** intraday, its eighth straight losing session. Jefferies cut its target to ₹1,145 (from ₹1,210) and JM Financial to ₹1,025 (from ₹1,175); both kept Buy. Jefferies said credit costs could stay elevated for **one to two more quarters** as stress builds in Maharashtra, Karnataka and Telangana. ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/shriram-finance-stock-hits-11-month-low-as-growth-outlook-cut-credit-costs-weigh-brokerages-slash-target-price-14059892.html))
- **Five-Star Business Finance:** Q2 PAT was **₹299 cr (+4.61%)**, but that includes a **₹19 cr one-time tax benefit**, and PBT fell 2% to ₹375 cr. Management expects its cost of funds to rise "amid the tightening rate cycle". Rate increases on new loans took effect from October to protect NIM, which held at 19.87%. The stock closed at **₹414.85 (−7.45%)** on BSE. ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/stocks/five-star-business-finance-share-price-q2-results-net-profit-up-5-on-higher-nii-aum-expands-14-20019803.htm))
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** this quarter, investors are paying for the credit-cost line and the funding-cost outlook more than for headline PAT. MFL has no listed equity, but its NCD investors and the agencies read the same signals. In MFL's Q2 pack, show credit cost and the one-offs separately, and put the cost-of-funds trajectory next to them, before someone else frames them.

**2. MFL's own Tranche I NCD is at 0.99× its ₹200 cr base after Monday's bidding. Separately, CareEdge Global moved MFL's subsidiary Muthoot Microfin to a Positive outlook and explicitly credited support from MFL.**
- **Tranche I:** subscribed **0.99×** at 26 Oct, 16:58. Retail is 0.75×, HNI 1.36×, non-institutional 0.80× and institutional 0.00×. That is up from 0.55× on Friday. The issue is **₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe**, allotted first-come first-served, and closes **Thu 5 Nov**. ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/))
- **Muthoot Microfin:** CareEdge Global reaffirmed **CareEdge BB-** and revised the outlook to **Positive from Stable** on its **USD 50 mn ECB programme** (USD 15 mn existing, including a GIFT City-listed ECB, plus USD 35 mn proposed). The agency said the rating factors in "expected support from parent Muthoot Fincorp Limited, which holds a **50.2%** stake" and "the parent's enhanced financial flexibility to provide support". Cited data: GNPA was **3.7%** at 30 Jun 2026, against 4.8% at Mar-25, and FY26 credit cost was 3.0% (7.5% in FY25). ([EquityBulls](https://www.equitybulls.com/news/377689/muthoot-microfin-earns-positive-outlook-from-careedge-global-on-stronger-asset-q), [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/muthootmicrofin_26102026101916_MMLCareEdgeGlobalPressReleaseSD.pdf))
- Despite the outlook change, Muthoot Microfin shares traded at **₹147.40, down more than 4%**, at 11:05 IST. ([BusinessLine live](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-26-october-2026/article71622964.ece))
- **Why it matters for MFL:**
  - On the NCD, the base is close to covered with eight bidding days left, so the question now is how much of the ₹300 cr green shoe gets taken up.
  - On the rating, an outside agency has written down MFL's support capacity as a reason to view the subsidiary more positively. That supports MFL's group-support narrative. It also confirms that agencies rating the subsidiary assume MFL would support it, which is a contingent call on MFL's capital.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Tuesday: Gold loan sector

### The stress is starting at the borrower, and the gold isn't the problem yet

**1. "Secured" no longer automatically means "safe".** Moneycontrol's 26 Oct explainer pulls the Q2 warnings together. ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/commodities/why-are-nbfcs-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans-despite-a-rush-to-expand-14060075.html))
- Shriram (23 Oct) joined other NBFCs in flagging a rise in gold-loan bad loans, and delinquencies are higher in **EMI-based** gold-loan accounts.
- **Bajaj Finance's Rajeev Jain** prefers a "longer tenor bullet model" over short-tenor, high-LTV schemes, "even if it comes at a slower growth".
- **Piramal's Jairam Sridharan** estimated that **15–23%** of FY26 industry gold-loan disbursements, around **₹4.7 lakh cr**, went to borrowers who were 90+ days overdue on at least one other loan. He added that Piramal's own exposure to such borrowers is under 1% of its October disbursements, and that the figure does not mean those loans have gone bad. ([ET, 22 Oct](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/india-gold-loan-boom-rbi-regulations-borrower-stress-default-risks-nbfc-growth/articleshow/135113574.cms))

**2. The book is still growing fast** ([ET, 22 Oct](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/india-gold-loan-boom-rbi-regulations-borrower-stress-default-risks-nbfc-growth/articleshow/135113574.cms)):

| Metric | Figure | Source note |
|---|---|---|
| Bank loans against gold jewellery | ₹5.52 lakh cr, +88.1% YoY (31 Jul 2026) | RBI data, via ET |
| NBFC gold loans | ~₹3.6 lakh cr, +69% YoY (31 Aug) | Reuters, via ET |
| Share of non-housing retail lending | ~28% (Macquarie) | via ET/TOI |
| Market forecast | >₹30 lakh cr by Mar-2028 (ICRA) | via ET |

**3. New entrants are adding capacity just as the cycle turns** (all via [ET, 22 Oct](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/india-gold-loan-boom-rbi-regulations-borrower-stress-default-risks-nbfc-growth/articleshow/135113574.cms)):
- Tata Capital (88.6% of Yogakshemam Loans) and Godrej Capital (Kanakadurga's gold book) bought their way in during July.
- Aditya Birla Capital is targeting 200–300 gold branches by Mar-27; L&T Finance plans 500 this year.
- **The RBI framework in force since 1 Apr 2026:** LTV of 85% up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for ₹2.5–5 lakh and 75% above that, with consumption bullet loans capped at **12 months**.

**4. Collateral is the second line of defence, and it held this week.**
- Gold is around 5% off its October record (Brickwork's Rajeev Sharan) and sits just above $4,000/oz. ([BusinessLine live](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-26-october-2026/article71622964.ece))
- Macquarie's warning is about a *combination*: a sharp price fall alongside rising defaults, which forces bulk auctions and weaker realisations. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/india-gold-loan-boom-rbi-regulations-borrower-stress-default-risks-nbfc-growth/articleshow/135113574.cms))

**What it means for MFL:**
- **Cross-default screen.** Piramal's "90+ DPD elsewhere" metric will become an analyst question. Before the Q2 call, MFL should know its own share of FY26 and H1 FY27 disbursements that went to customers overdue elsewhere, using bureau data. (That is my suggestion; the figure isn't public.)
- **Product mix.** Bajaj and the Moneycontrol piece both point to EMI and short-tenor gold products as the stress pocket. Cut MFL's Stage 2 and 30+ figures by repayment type (bullet vs EMI). This links directly to today's 7 AM teaching on NPA upgradation.
- **Competition.** More gold branches from ABCL, L&T, Tata and Godrej means pricing pressure in exactly the southern markets where MFL holds **62.33 t** of pledged gold (Mar-26, per [CARE](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202610121058_Muthoot_Fincorp_Limited.pdf)).
- **Unverified item:** a bankcreds.com post claims Manappuram and Muthoot Finance "jump 8% on October 27" after a gold duty hike. Exchange data shows Manappuram +0.49% on 26 Oct, and markets haven't opened on the 27th. It is **unverified and not used**.
