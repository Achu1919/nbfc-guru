# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Friday, October 30, 2026

*Friday, Oct 30, 2026 edition · News & Markets desk. Indian prices are Thursday's (29 Oct) close. MCX gold is this morning's 07:08 IST print, spot gold is the 07:01 IST quote, and Brent is Thursday's settlement. Regulatory headlines only; the deep dive is in the Rules report.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. MFL's Tranche I NCD reached 1.67× on Thursday. HNI demand is above 2.3× and retail has crossed 1.4×.**
- Subscription was **1.67×** at 29 Oct, 17:00: retail 1.42×, HNI 2.32×, non-institutional 1.10×, institutional 0.00×. It was 1.45× a day earlier. The issue is **₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe**, allotted first-come first-served, and closes **5 Nov**. ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/))
- **Why it matters for Muthoot Fincorp (MFL):** roughly ₹334 cr is in against the ₹500 cr full size (1.67 × ₹200 cr). The daily run-rate is about 0.2× (≈₹40–45 cr), so the full green shoe needs that pace to hold for the remaining bidding days. Non-institutional crossing 1× is new since Wednesday.

**2. Small NBFCs have lost an estimated ₹300 cr to cyberattacks in two months, mostly through payment and disbursement APIs.**
- ET, cited by Moneycontrol on 29 Oct, reports about **150 complaints/FIRs**. Targets are mostly NBFCs with turnover of about ₹1,000 cr or less; one Delhi lender reportedly lost ₹7.5 cr and then ₹7.25 cr days later. The ₹300 cr is a sourced estimate, not an official figure. ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/rs-300-crore-stolen-from-small-nbfcs-in-cyberattacks-officials-flag-jamtara-style-fraud-risk-14063062.html), [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/ai-powered-cyberattacks-siphon-300-crore-from-small-nbfcs-in-two-months/articleshow/135278215.cms))
- CERT-In's 9 Oct advisory describes attackers stealing gateway and partner-bank credentials, then calling disbursement APIs from whitelisted infrastructure to skip KYC, sanction and limit checks. Its fixes are credential rotation, MFA, maker-checker and payout limits. ([CERT-In advisory summary](https://www.compliedai.com/updates/cert-in/ciad-2026-0046-threat-actors-exploiting-payment-and-disbursement-apis-fo-cabo4h))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** MFL is far bigger than the targets, but it disburses gold loans digitally and across a large branch network, which means a lot of payout traffic through bank APIs. As a Middle Layer NBFC it falls under RBI's IT governance directions. A quick check that disbursement-API keys are rotated and payout velocity limits are in place is cheap insurance.

**3. Rate-hike talk got louder. US inflation came in hot, gold fell and PNB Housing is raising lending rates.**
- US PCE prices rose **0.5%** in September. CME FedWatch now prices about an **83% chance of a December Fed hike**. Spot gold fell about 1% on Thursday. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/29/gold-slips-as-markets-assess-fed-signals-await-us-inflation-data.html))
- Spot gold was **$4,006.44 (−0.79%)** at 07:01 IST today, and MCX gold was **₹1,42,351/10g (−0.17%)** at 07:08 IST. ([exa.ai XAUUSD](https://exa.ai/library/markets/commodity/gold?currency=USD), [Makro](https://makro.live/mcx-gold-silver-price))
- PNB Housing will raise lending rates across tenors from **1 Nov**. CEO Ajai Shukla said a 25 bp increase hasn't slowed business and that a 75–100 bp rise in FY27 is unlikely. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/no-business-impact-from-rate-hike-100-bps-rise-unlikely-in-fy27-ajai-shukla/article71641156.ece))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** lenders are passing higher costs to borrowers before the RBI moves. MFL can use that cover to review its own gold-loan rate card. Gold at about $4,000 and below ₹1.43 lakh/10g on MCX keeps LTV headroom tight on loans written near the peak.

**4. Equitas SFB's MD & CEO P N Vasudevan resigned over a "perceived conflict of interest". The stock fell 20%.**
- His daughter co-promotes Unico Housing Finance, an RBI-licensed NBFC. He says there were no dealings beyond one repaid short-term deposit, and that the link was disclosed to the board and RBI. He stays on until a successor is in place. Equitas closed at **₹52.11 (−20%)** on NSE. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/equitas-sfb-md-amp-ceo-p-n-vasudevan-resigns-citing-perception-of-conflict-of-interest-stock-down-20/article71640049.ece))
- **Why it matters for MFL:** investors priced a governance perception, not a breach, at 20% of market value. MFL filed for a ₹3,000 cr IPO in August ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/indias-muthoot-fincorp-files-30-bln-rupee-ipo-2026-08-13/)). For a promoter-group company heading to listing, related-party and group-entity disclosures are where investor questions will land first.

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Friday: Auto finance & EV lending

**1. Two-wheeler demand is strong, EVs most of all.** Hero MotoCorp's Q2 FY27 call (28 Oct):
- Industry two-wheeler retail grew **27%** in Q2, with ICE +23% and EV +82%.
- Hero's EV wholesale rose **98%**, and Vida retail rose 74% to **66,000** units.
- Management said rural demand is "as good as, if not better than" urban, even with drought pockets.
- EV capacity doubled to **30,000/month**, aiming for 45,000 by Q4. The whole industry is supply-constrained.
- Hero raised prices by about **₹1,000/unit** from 1 Oct, and the stock fell more than 4% on margin commentary.
- ([Cofacto call summary](https://cofacto.ai/market-news/heromotoco-q2-fy27-earnings-call), [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/companies/hero-motocoro-not-cutting-down-on-investment-to-protect-margins-ceo-harshvardhan-chitale/article71639561.ece), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/fiis-turn-net-sellers-at-rs944-crore-as-sensex-jumps-905-points-20022757.htm))

**2. EV lenders are scaling and specialising.**
- Shriram Finance's EV disbursements are about **₹350 cr/month**, up from ₹30–40 cr at the start, with a ₹5,000 cr book target. Last Friday's edition cited ~₹300 cr. ([Business Standard, 25 Oct](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/people/shriram-finance-eyes-20-000-crore-gold-loan-book-bets-big-on-ev-growth-126102500447_1.html))
- Greaves Finance named Mukul Kanchan, formerly CFO at Plum and at Clix Capital before that, as ED & CEO to scale **ev.fin**, which finances e-two-wheelers at the point of sale in 86 cities. ([Greaves press release, 13 Oct](https://greavescotton.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GCL_GFL_PRsd_removed.pdf))
- Astranova's Novafusion Capital (licence reported last week) is again in the news, targeting ₹5,000 cr of AUM in EV fleet and gig-driver lending. ([RDU Global, 29 Oct](https://rduglobal.live/article/2026/10/29/astranova-mobility-arm-wins-rbi-nbfc-licence-sets-rs-5-000-crore-aum-g-8691))

**3. Vehicle financiers keep growing in a rising-rate year.** TVS Credit's Q2 (reported 28 Oct): PAT ₹243 cr (+19%), AUM ₹33,818 cr (+22%), disbursements +31%. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/tvs-credit-reports-19-growth-in-q2-pat-at-243-crore/article71632336.ece))

**4. Read for Muthoot Fincorp:**
- **Cross-sell:** Shriram is the clearest case of a lender running vehicle finance and gold loans side by side, targeting a ₹20,000 cr gold book. It's the competitive template to watch in MFL's semi-urban and rural branches.
- **Rural cash flow:** strong rural two-wheeler demand is good for MFL's customers. It also means more of their monthly income goes to fixed EMIs, so stress shows up first as slower interest servicing on bullet gold loans.
- **Battery collateral:** EV lending prices battery and residual-value risk that no one has a long data history on. A gold loan carries none of that collateral uncertainty.
